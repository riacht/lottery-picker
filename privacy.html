Privacy Policy

Lottery Number Picker

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This app is a local number-picking helper for lottery-style games. It does not sell lottery tickets and is not affiliated with any official lottery operator.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data on our servers. The app does not require an account.

Data created in the app (saved number combinations, optional photos, settings) is stored only on your device.

Photos

If you choose to attach a photo of a ticket or a results screenshot, the image stays on your device and is linked only to that saved entry. Camera or file access is used only when you start that action.

Internet

The app may open official lottery result pages in your browser when you tap a results link. That browsing is handled by your device/browser, not by our servers.

Permissions

  • Camera / photos (optional): only to attach an image you choose.
  • No obligatory tracking or advertising permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised.
